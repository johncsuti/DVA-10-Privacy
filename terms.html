2. What Sophia does

Sophia is a Discord moderation and anti-scam application designed to help server administrators detect and respond to abusive or deceptive content. Features may include:

  • scanning messages and supported attachments for scam and abuse indicators;
  • extracting text from images using OCR;
  • detecting and decoding QR codes when reasonably possible;
  • analyzing URLs, domains, Discord invite links, prize claims, impersonation patterns, cryptocurrency or wallet lures, and related scam indicators;
  • using exact and perceptual image hashes to identify known or substantially similar scam imagery;
  • sending uncertain cases to authorized human moderators;
  • deleting content or taking other moderation actions when server configuration permits;
  • maintaining limited moderation state necessary to prevent duplicate or conflicting actions.

Features may change over time. Sophia is intended to assist moderation; it does not replace competent human judgment, common sense, Discord's own safety systems, or the ancient administrative technique known as "looking at what actually happened."

4. Automated moderation, mistakes, and human review

Sophia may use deterministic rules, image hashes, OCR results, QR analysis, URL/domain checks, reputation signals, configuration data, and other security indicators to classify content. Depending on server configuration and confidence level, Sophia may automatically remove content or take other configured moderation actions.

Automated systems can produce false positives, false negatives, incomplete results, decode failures, network failures, OCR mistakes, and other errors. Sophia may therefore send uncertain material to human moderators instead of automatically enforcing against it.

A Sophia classification such as Clean, Review, or Scam is an operational moderation assessment. It is not a statement that a person has committed a crime, breached a contract, achieved villain status, or otherwise been found legally responsible for anything.

Server administrators remain responsible for the moderation policies and final enforcement environment in their communities.

5. Acceptable use and things you absolutely should not do

You may use Sophia for legitimate moderation, security, anti-abuse, testing, and administrative purposes. You may not use Sophia in a way that is unlawful, abusive, deceptive, or intended to interfere with the service.

Do not attack or sabotage the service
  • Do not intentionally overload Sophia, flood commands, manufacture abusive traffic, or attempt denial-of-service behavior.
  • Do not exploit vulnerabilities, bypass authorization checks, access another server's moderation information, or attempt to obtain credentials or private infrastructure data.
  • Do not deliberately submit malicious files or pathological content solely to exhaust CPU, memory, storage, OCR, image decoders, network bandwidth, or moderator patience.
Do not weaponize moderation
  • Do not knowingly configure Sophia to target people for unlawful harassment or discrimination.
  • Do not intentionally mislabel benign content as malicious in order to poison learned moderation data.
  • Do not attempt to manipulate review workflows, impersonate authorized moderators, or cause repeated punishment of the same event.
