1. Scope and who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to Sophia MOD ("Sophia," "the application," "we," "us," or "our") when Sophia is installed in or used with a Discord server. It describes how data is processed when Sophia performs moderation, anti-scam scanning, moderator review, abuse prevention, auditing, and related security functions.

Sophia is an independent third-party Discord application. Discord is a separate service with its own terms and privacy practices. This policy governs Sophia's processing and does not replace Discord's own policies.

Server owners and administrators decide whether to install and configure Sophia in their communities. By participating in a server where Sophia has been enabled, messages and attachments you post in channels Sophia is permitted to access may be processed for moderation and security purposes.

2. Information Sophia may process

Sophia practices data minimization, but moderation cannot work without processing the content being moderated. Depending on configuration and the event being analyzed, Sophia may process the following:

Discord identifiers and metadata
  • Discord user IDs and usernames/display names available with the message;
  • server (guild), channel, thread where applicable, and message IDs;
  • message timestamps and moderation-event timestamps;
  • message URLs or jump links used by moderators to locate evidence;
  • basic attachment metadata such as file name, type, size, and source URL.
Message and attachment content
  • text contained in messages Sophia is authorized to inspect;
  • image attachments and other supported image content;
  • URLs, domains, invite links, and other link-like content contained in messages or images;
  • text extracted from images using optical character recognition (OCR);
  • QR-code content when a QR code can be reliably detected and decoded.
Derived moderation and security data
  • cryptographic hashes and perceptual image hashes used to recognize exact or visually similar scam images;
  • rule matches, scam indicators, confidence/score information, moderation verdicts, and reasons;
  • whether an item was classified as clean, scam, or requiring human review;
  • moderator review decisions and enforcement state needed to avoid duplicate or conflicting actions;
  • limited diagnostic information necessary to troubleshoot scanner failures or security incidents.

Sophia is not designed to collect passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information unrelated to moderation. Users should never post such information in Discord messages.

3. Why Sophia processes this information

Sophia processes the information described above only for legitimate moderation, safety, security, and operational purposes, including:

  • detecting phishing, impersonation, fraudulent giveaways, cryptocurrency/wallet scams, malicious or deceptive links, fake prize claims, and related abuse;
  • identifying exact or substantially similar images previously associated with scams;
  • extracting visible text and URLs from image-based scams that would otherwise evade text-only moderation;
  • sending uncertain cases to authorized human moderators for review instead of automatically punishing a user;
  • removing or restricting content confidently identified as malicious when server configuration permits;
  • preventing duplicate enforcement and maintaining restart-safe moderation state;
  • investigating abuse, diagnosing failures, protecting the application, and maintaining service reliability.

Sophia does not use message-content data for targeted advertising, commercial behavioral profiling, or the training of machine-learning or generative-AI models.

4. Why Message Content access is necessary

Sophia is a passive moderation system. Scam accounts do not voluntarily invoke moderation commands before posting malicious material. To perform its core function, Sophia must be able to inspect ordinary messages and attachments in channels where server administrators have authorized it to operate.

This includes analyzing message text, attachment images, extracted OCR text, QR-code data, and links for scam indicators. Requiring every user to mention Sophia or invoke a slash command would defeat the anti-scam purpose of the service.

5. Automated moderation and human review

Sophia uses deterministic rules, hashes, OCR, link/domain checks, and related security signals to classify content. Some server configurations may permit Sophia to automatically remove content when the available evidence strongly indicates a scam.

Content that is uncertain or cannot be analyzed reliably may be referred to authorized moderators for human review. Human reviewers can classify the content and determine whether moderation action is appropriate.

Automated systems are imperfect. A classification is a moderation/security assessment, not a legal finding about a user or their intent.

6. Data retention

For production operation, Sophia's standard policy is to retain raw message content and content-derived evidence such as OCR text, decoded QR content, and attachment references for no longer than 30 days unless longer retention is reasonably necessary for an active moderation case, abuse or security investigation, legal obligation, dispute, or a user-requested review.

Minimal security and moderation records may be retained longer where doing so is necessary to prevent repeated abuse, preserve the integrity of moderation actions, or recognize previously confirmed scam campaigns. Examples may include image hashes, message/user/channel identifiers associated with moderation actions, action state, timestamps, and moderator decisions.

Data is deleted or de-identified when it is no longer reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was retained. Server administrators may also remove Sophia from their server, which prevents future collection from that server.

7. When information may be disclosed

We do not sell personal information or message content. We may disclose limited data only when reasonably necessary in the following circumstances:

  • Authorized server moderators: moderation evidence may be displayed to moderators responsible for reviewing or enforcing server rules;
  • Infrastructure and service providers: limited data may be processed by providers necessary to host or operate Sophia, subject to appropriate access controls;
  • Security and abuse response: information may be used or disclosed as reasonably necessary to investigate attacks, fraud, abuse, or threats to users, Discord communities, Sophia, or its infrastructure;
  • Legal requirements: information may be preserved or disclosed when required by applicable law, valid legal process, or to protect legal rights and safety.

Sophia does not provide message content to advertisers or data brokers.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards intended to protect information processed by Sophia. These controls include restricted access to moderation data, separation of moderation functions from public interfaces, encrypted network transport where supported, least-privilege access, and operational logging for security and reliability.

Production systems that store Message Content data must use encryption at rest at the storage, filesystem, volume, database, or equivalent infrastructure layer before Sophia is placed into production with such storage enabled.

No system can guarantee perfect security. Users and server administrators should report suspected security issues promptly.

9. User choices, opt-out, access, and deletion

Individual opt-out

Individual users generally cannot opt out of moderation scanning while continuing to post in a server and channel where the server's administrators have enabled Sophia. Allowing individual opt-out would permit malicious users to bypass the server's anti-scam controls.

Server owners and administrators can disable Sophia's access to channels or remove the application from the server, which stops future processing in those locations.

Access or deletion requests

Users may request deletion of stored activity data associated with their Discord account by emailing privacy@coolcomputers.info. Include your Discord user ID so the relevant records can be identified. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, or other account credentials.

We may request reasonable information needed to verify that a request concerns the correct account. After verification, we will delete or de-identify applicable stored activity data unless retention is reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, an active moderation dispute, compliance with law, or another legitimate obligation.

Appeals and server moderation

Questions about a specific server moderation decision should generally be directed to that server's moderators or administrators. Sophia may provide technical evidence, but individual communities control their own rules and moderation policies.

10. Children and age-restricted use

Sophia is not directed to children and does not intentionally seek to collect information from children for advertising, profiling, or commercial purposes. Use of Discord is subject to Discord's own age requirements and policies. If you believe information has been processed in a manner that requires special handling because of age, contact us using the privacy contact below.

11. Processing location and international users

Sophia may be operated or hosted in the United States. Users accessing Discord from other jurisdictions should understand that moderation and security data may therefore be processed in the United States or another location used by necessary infrastructure providers, subject to applicable safeguards and law.

12. Third-party services and links

Messages analyzed by Sophia may contain links to third-party websites or services. The presence of a link in moderation evidence does not mean we endorse or control that third party. Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

Discord itself is a separate platform. Users should review Discord's applicable privacy and developer policies for information about Discord's own handling of data.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Sophia's features, data practices, legal obligations, or security controls change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page identifies the current version. Material changes will be reflected on this page before or when the changed practice takes effect, as appropriate.

14. Privacy contact

For privacy questions, access/deletion requests, or concerns about Sophia's handling of data:

Email: privacy@coolcomputers.info

For a deletion request, include your Discord user ID and enough context for us to identify the relevant records. Never send a Discord token, password, two-factor authentication code, or other login credential.
